## 📖 Content Covered

### Main Concepts
- **Newton's Second Law**: F = ma
  - Force is proportional to acceleration
  - Acceleration = Force / Mass
  - This is the foundation of ALL mechanical systems

- **System Elements**:
  - **Mass (M)**: Resists acceleration (inertia)
  - **Damper (B)**: Opposes velocity (friction, air resistance)
  - **Spring (K)**: Opposes displacement (restoring force)

- **Mathematical Model**:
  ```
  F(t) = M(d²x/dt²) + B(dx/dt) + Kx
  Where:
  - F(t) = Applied force
  - x(t) = Position/displacement
  - dx/dt = Velocity
  - d²x/dt² = Acceleration
  ```

## 💡 Key Insights

### From Teacher
- Newton's law is the starting point for ALL mechanical analysis
- Each physical component (mass, spring, damper) adds a term to the equation
- The mathematical model predicts how the system will behave

### My Understanding
- **Mass**: "Lazy" - doesn't want to change motion
- **Spring**: "Pushy" - always wants to return to original position  
- **Damper**: "Resistor" - opposes any movement

### Real-World Connections
- **Car Suspension**: Mass=car, Spring=suspension spring, Damper=shock absorber
- **Building in Earthquake**: Mass=building, Spring=structural flexibility, Damper=damping materials
- **Robot Arm**: Mass=arm weight, Spring=joint stiffness, Damper=motor friction
